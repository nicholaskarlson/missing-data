Censored Event Times

Fictional B8-T03: withdrawal before transfer by twelve months among the original 1,600 invitees under the actual policy mixture.

Withdrawal is the event; transfer competes; withdrawn consent leaves a blank outcome. Administrative follow-up through month twelve establishes a known binary zero when no event occurs.

Run python scripts/check_unit10.py --base-r --outdir build/unit10-review from the companion root after setup, using a new output directory.

Inspect unit10_stdlib.csv, unit10_stdlib_people.csv and unit10_stdlib_risksets.csv. Three routes compare summaries, original histories and pre-exit risk sets. Standardization uses original group shares 610/1600, 609/1600, 381/1600. Recorded clocks are integer thousandths; tied censors remain in the event denominator.

The standardized estimate is 0.1611101690; logical support is 0.160625 to 0.167500. A conditional representative-history censoring link and follow-up support are needed. Numerical agreement does not validate them. No confidence interval or causal no-transfer effect is supplied.

Separate complete teaching fixtures show tied exits and failure under selective censoring; these are not hidden Tonmick records.
