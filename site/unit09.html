Randomized at entry, selected at follow-up

The sign remains unresolved. A positive selected-group difference does not identify the offer effect. This case retains partial answers and uses a conservative randomization confidence procedure without a response model.

Question and target

B8-T02 is the mean offer-versus-no-offer potential full wellbeing-score effect in the 1,219 originally randomized baseline respondents, regardless of uptake. Five one-to-five items are scored at twelve months, reversing wb4. Original college shares N_c/1,219 remain fixed. This target excludes nonrandomized invitees and is distinct from the effect of mentoring uptake or latent trajectories.

Assume meaningful bounded potential scores under a defined reference measurement regime, consistency, no interference and faithful original assignment. Conditional on its observed arm counts, assignment is uniform within each college and independent across colleges. It is not rerandomized among later respondents. These are required causal/design conditions; the source files alone cannot verify all of them in a real study.

Inputs and contract

  • Frame, original assignments and observed follow-up.
  • Target, bounds, design and uncertainty contract.
  • Pre-analysis freeze and prior-knowledge disclosure.

Run

Inspect

Open report.json, unit09_stdlib.csv and unit09_stdlib_people.csv. Compare arm/, pattern/, college/TC01/, realized/, causal_support/ and confidence/. All 684 summary values and 1,219 person rows are checked through independent NumPy, plain Python and base R. The checker reconstructs the 3,993 observed keyed cells from raw answers. Only the checker reads expected results.

There are 825 twelve-month respondents, but only 701 full wellbeing scores. There are 214 people returning after an earlier missed wave. Pattern 101 supplies a twelve-month response even though month 6 was missed. S1001841's raw answers are blank,1,1,4,2; reversing wb4 yields observed sum 6 and full-score limits 1.4 to 2.2. The incomplete full score stays blank.

Three ranges with different meanings

  • Realized statistic D: the college-weighted full assigned-arm contrast, if all realized answers were known, is between -1.281362 and 1.478587 from item support alone. It is not exact causal truth for this assignment.
  • Finite causal support: allowing unseen counterfactual scores as well gives -2.640197 to 2.739295. These are logical limits, not confidence limits.
  • Randomization confidence: a range-based Neyman variance upper bound 0.013301408052805181 gives Chebyshev radius 0.515779 at alpha .05. Expanding D's support interval and clipping to [-4,4] gives -1.797141 to 1.994366. Under the stated conditions, the procedure has at-least-95% coverage over original assignment conditional on arm counts. It is not a posterior probability or a normal approximation.

The full-outcome variance formula subtracts an unidentifiable nonnegative effect-variance term. Drop that term and bound either potential-score variance by 4N_c/(N_c−1). Sum w_c squared times that bound times (1/n_c1+1/n_c0), then take sqrt(Vmax/.05). Because the observed support interval contains full-data D for every valid response pattern, the confidence expansion needs no MAR model. See the numerical contract for the complete derivation and Liu and Yang's Section 2 for the exact full-data design mean/variance.

Audit the claim

The standardized complete-case difference is 0.095622; it describes selected full-score respondents. Its sign and closeness to the pooled difference 0.097813 do not validate a causal conclusion. Equal response rates would not prove ignorability. Do not substitute uptake or latent effects for the offer-score target.

All-known realized scores collapse D's missing-data range but leave counterfactual/design uncertainty. All-missing scores give [-4,4] limits; undefined complete-case estimates are omitted, not coded zero. Duplicate or missing IDs/waves, invalid response/item values and corrupted or unbalanced assignments refuse. New data collection must respect consent and measurement comparability. A later reveal supplies a realized-score benchmark, not unavailable paired item-score causal truth.
