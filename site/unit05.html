NEKpress Research: Proof-First Guides

Complete the data, keep the assumptions

Model audit: warning. This the numerical companion case reproduces a bounded working-model calculation. Its conditional-independence/ignorable-observation account raises a substantial residual-correlation warning. A successful check does not validate the model or correct MNAR.

Question and target

B8-T01 is the equal-person full baseline stress mean among all 1,600 Tonmick invitees. Five raw answers lie between one and five; reverse st4 before scoring. The 5,934 observed cells remain fixed; 2,066 missing cells are completed. Partial respondents and nonrespondents stay in the roster. This finite-roster target has zero complete-data uncertainty when all answers are known.

Inputs and assumptions

  • Invitation frame and observed raw items, joined by student_id.
  • Frozen model/variance/failure contract.
  • Indexed primitive uniforms and prefit freeze.

The four groups are part_time then first_gen. Each group/item has a categorical distribution on keyed 1 through 5, independent across items conditional on group and parameters. Each vector has an independent Dirichlet(1,1,1,1,1) prior. Joint survey/item observation is assumed ignorable given the group, with distinct response parameters. Neither that assumption nor conditional independence follows from the observed data. Latent-stress-dependent response in the documented teaching World remains a vulnerability.

Run

Follow setup for Python 3.10–3.12 and NumPy 1.26.4. Choose new output folders:

python scripts/check_unit05.py --numeric --outdir build/unit05-python
python scripts/check_unit05.py --base-r --outdir build/unit05-three-route

The second command uses base R without add-ons. No World checkout or renderer is required. Independent routes use scalar incomplete-Gamma inversion, NumPy density integration, and R qgamma. The analyzers read no expected answers.

Inspect

Open report.json, unit05_stdlib.csv and unit05_stdlib_people.csv. Inspect cell/11/st1/, draw/1/theta/11/st1/, example/, posterior/, MC/ and diagnostic/. The 4,378 summary values and 32,000 person rows are compared at the unchanged absolute/relative tolerance of 1e-11. Every observed raw cell is copied exactly in twenty rosters: 118,680 preserved observations per route.

S1000218 has raw answers blank, 4, 3, 5, 4. Its group-11 st1 counts are 18,45,62,39,17; posterior parameters are 19,46,63,40,18. Its first cell uniform 0.430563 selects category 3. The correct completed score is 3.0 after reversing raw st4=5 to keyed 1. In the raw output, st4 must remain 5.

Three different kinds of uncertainty

  • Logical support: Unit 1's 2.44675 to 3.47975 follows from observed items and one-to-five support, without a completion model.
  • Model-conditional posterior: exact mean 2.952424, variance 0.000059661348415486515, standard deviation 0.007724. Chebyshev's inequality gives a conservative at-least-95% posterior bound 2.917881 to 2.986967, not a central interval or established coverage claim.
  • Monte Carlo: twenty completions average 2.955163, with estimated Monte Carlo SE 0.001610 (exact-model counterpart 0.001727). More independent completions stabilize that average; they do not narrow the posterior uncertainty. The analytic mean has no simulation error.

Shared probability-vector uncertainty contributes to the missing-sum variance. For one group/item, missing count m, posterior concentration A and predictive category variance v give variance m(A+m)v/(A+1). Sum the independent group/item variances and divide by 8,000 squared. Do not borrow the regression pilot's completed-data slope variance or stack the completions as observed students.

Audit the claim

The largest within-complete-score, group-centered item correlation is 0.510137, above the predeclared descriptive warning threshold 0.10. This is not a calibrated test. Selection into complete records may contribute, so it cannot identify whether the item model or observation assumption failed. Preserve the warning and the wider logical bounds. The narrow posterior calculation is not an established scientific correction or proof of MAR.

Invalid inputs, malformed/open-interval primitives, observed-cell changes and ambiguous categorical boundaries stop execution. All-known target answers produce a known mean and zero missing-answer variance without fake repeated completions. Future joint models, inclusive withdrawal regression, FCS, sensitivity, coverage and reveal remain separate work.
