Book 8 companion / Unit 6

Chained equations need a joint story

Numerical companion. This is a two-item binary diagnostic and fixed-parameter kernel audit. It does not repair the full five-item model or validate response assumptions.

Target and input

B8-D06 is the proportion of all 1,600 fictional invitees whose raw baseline st1 and st2 answers would both be 4 or 5. Preserve the raw answers and unknown binary indicators separately. Coarsening cannot reconstruct missing ordinal values. The frame and observed baseline CSVs are the only ordinary data.

Run

Follow the setup, then run python scripts/check_unit06.py --base-r --outdir build/unit06-audit with a new folder. No private World checkout or renderer is required.

Inspect

The summary CSV records the exact finite-mixture moments, allocation weights, twenty joint draws and both update orders. The people CSV retains 1,600 raw/probability records; the separate completed CSV has 32,000 binary rows and preserves measured categories. An event is always recomputed as A times B.

Audit the claim

Inspect toy/incompatible/: both positive chains converge, but their joint high-high probabilities differ by order despite equal marginal means. Fixed-table compatibility does not establish equivalence for generic refitted FCS. The Tonmick posterior is conditional on a strong unverified observation model. Twenty direct completions are not used as an exact finite-chain reference. Unit 5 retains its scientific warning. All 9,193 summary values and every person/completion row are checked through three independent routes.
