Planned Missingness and Reporting

B8-D12 is the finite full five-item wellbeing mean among 956 six-month respondents; all 1,219 randomized students remain in the audit. Reverse wb4. The conditional design assumes independent uniform forms, fixed reference answers and no form effect on responses or roster membership. Recorded form frequencies do not verify these conditions.

The ten-item ledger separates 7,429 observed answers, 1,912 planned omissions, 219 incidental blanks and 2,630 wave-missing cells. Scheduled questions fall by20%; no percentage precision-loss claim follows.

Run python scripts/check_unit12.py --base-r --outdir build/unit12-review from the companion root after setup, with a fresh output directory. Inspect 45 summary keys and the people/cells/masks files: 1,219 people, 40 form/item cells, 243 public hypothetical allocations. Planned raw blanks remain blank; H fields are blank for wave nonrespondents.

H uses weights 1 on common items and 1.5 on scheduled rotated wellbeing items, divided by 5. Bound incidental scheduled blanks by 1 and 5. The unavailable-statistic interval is 3.453975–3.585774. A pair-square variance bound and Chebyshev expansion give 3.372155–3.667594 with at least 95% coverage under the declared conditional form law, even with arbitrary incidental loss. This is not a posterior interval, an all-randomized mean interval or an offer effect. Respondent full-score logical support is 2.967573–3.869665; all-randomized support is 2.543068–4.113536.

Three independent routes and a raw reconstruction checker reproduce the calculation. Teaching fixtures verify unbiasedness, mixed variance 13/400, available-mean bias and the effect of changing the allocation rule. An actual protocol and stable measurement conditions remain needed.
