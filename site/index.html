What the companion checks

Independent routes reconstruct the specified calculations from shared raw inputs and primitive random draws. Checkers compare the resulting values and records with frozen references. Agreement does not establish a missingness assumption or make a model adequate.

Unit 5 retains its model-audit warning. The sensitivity grids and conditional design results retain their stated limits.
